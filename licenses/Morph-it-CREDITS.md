# Morph-it! credits — CINQUE Dictionary v3

The word-list `data/lexicon-v3.js` is an adapted compilation which incorporates word forms from **Morph-it! v0.4.8**, by **Marco Baroni and Eros Zanchetta**, University of Bologna / SSLMIT (original corpus and morphological resource, 2004–2009). Morph-it! is dual-licensed CC BY-SA 2.0 / LGPL; CINQUE uses the **CC BY-SA 2.0** option for the derived dictionary data.

- Source: https://docs.sslmit.unibo.it/doku.php?id=resources:morph-it
- Derived normalized word list: https://github.com/FredrikBorgstrom/dict-it-it
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/
- Changes: selected only five-character alphabetic forms, lowercased and normalized accents, de-duplicated and merged with previous CINQUE vocabulary; excluded roman numerals from the prior dictionary and separated everyday solutions from permissible guesses.
